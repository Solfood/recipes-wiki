# DEC-0001: Adopt engineering scaffold for AI-assisted development

- Date: 2026-04-24
- Status: accepted
- Owner: Solfood
- Marker: RW-DX-0001
- Supersedes: N/A

## Context

The recipes-wiki is a static MkDocs site. Content additions are straightforward but improvements to the site structure, scripts, and CI occasionally need to be tracked. There is no existing work-tracking or session-continuity mechanism.

## Intake

- Objective: Add a lightweight scaffold for work-tracking and AI session continuity.
- In scope: CLAUDE.md, policies/project-policy.yaml, _engineering/work-index.md, _engineering/session-log.md, _engineering/decisions/ directory.
- Out of scope: Changes to MkDocs content in docs/, recipe files, mkdocs.yml, or CI workflows.
- Risk class: low
- Critical assets touched: none
- Dependencies: engineering-scaffold-template (Solfood/engineering-scaffold-template)
- Expected evidence: All scaffold files present, no placeholder strings remaining, `mkdocs build` still passes.

## Options Considered

1. **Place scaffold in docs/** — conflicts with MkDocs content; scaffold files would appear in the rendered site.
2. **Place scaffold in _engineering/** (chosen) — keeps scaffold fully separate from site content; MkDocs ignores the `_engineering/` directory.

## Decision

Adopt the scaffold with files under `_engineering/` to avoid any MkDocs conflicts.

## Why

A static wiki still benefits from session continuity — especially for AI-assisted content structure improvements and script maintenance. Keeping files out of `docs/` ensures they never appear in the rendered site.

## Risks & Mitigations

| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|-----------|
| _engineering/ files appear in MkDocs site | MkDocs only processes files under `docs_dir` (defaults to `docs/`); `_engineering/` is outside that scope |

## Rollback Plan

Delete CLAUDE.md, policies/, _engineering/. No MkDocs content or CI is affected.

## Evidence

Scaffold files created and verified under _engineering/. Policy risk tolerance set to low. Suite block references bluray and Willowbrook.
